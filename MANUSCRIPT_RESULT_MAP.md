section. It is an audit aid; numerical results remain in the generated tables,
not in this code-only repository.

## Important operational definitions

- The locked subtype labels are generated by script 05, not supplied as a
  hidden input.
- Unique SV burden counts one reciprocal BRASS `MATEID` pair once.
- Script 15 defines the seven gene pathways from gene-level SNV and SV events;
  chromosome-arm CNV and SV burden are represented separately as `CIN_ARM`
  and `SV_COMPLEX`. This exact definition should be used in the Methods.
- Script 16's “dual hit” is pathway-level convergence of two event classes. It
  does not establish biallelic inactivation of a single gene.
- Undefined CMS/CRPS/iCMS values are excluded from biological concordance
  statistics and summarized separately as classifier-callability categories.
- Script 21 analyses upstream CIBERSORTx, TIDE, ESTIMATE and oncoPredict
  outputs; it does not recreate those external algorithms.
- The minimal genomic classifier and the legacy genomic risk-score modules
  were deliberately excluded because their results are not used in the
  manuscript.
